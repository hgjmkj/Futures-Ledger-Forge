## 🎯 Why This Project Exists

Crypto futures trading on centralized exchanges is loud. Dashboards scream, leverage whispers sweet nothings, funding rates flicker like fireflies, and somewhere between the noise, a trader loses the thread of *why* they entered a position at 3:47 AM. Most journals capture the *what*. Almost none capture the *why* — and virtually none capture the *why not*.

**Auto-Crypto-Tradingjournal** was born from a simple, stubborn question: *What if your journal could argue back?*

Instead of passive logging, this project treats every trade as a case study, every drawdown as a dataset, and every winning streak as a hypothesis waiting to be falsified. The result is a journal that behaves less like a notebook and more like a **research lab** — one where the primary subject is you.

The project also bundles a **standalone trading course** (Beginner to Pro) so that newcomers aren't left to wander the futures desert alone. And for those who want to see machine reasoning in motion, the embedded **AI trader** acts as a co-pilot that studies, simulates, and critiques.

## 🧠 Core Philosophy

Four principles govern every line of code in this repository:

1. **Ownership Over Convenience** — Self-hosted means your data never leaves your perimeter. Your journal is a fortress, not a storefront.
2. **Depth Before Dashboards** — A pretty chart that teaches nothing is decoration. Every visualization here must answer a question.
3. **Discipline Is Measurable** — Emotional control isn't a mood; it's a metric. We quantify it.
4. **The Best Trade Is The One You Understand** — Clarity beats cleverness. Always.

These aren't slogans. They're enforced in the way data flows through the system — from raw execution feed to final insight.

## 📈 The Analytics Engine

Most analytics tools hand you a chart and wish you luck. This engine hands you a **conversation**.

### Layers of Insight

- **Micro Layer** — Per-trade anatomy: slippage, funding cost, time-in-trade, MAE/MFE curves.
- **Meso Layer** — Per-strategy cohorts: which setups outperform across volatility regimes.
- **Macro Layer** — Per-behavior patterns: tilt detection, revenge-trade signatures, overconfidence clusters.

### Signature Visualizations

- **Leverage Weather Map** — A heatmap showing how your leverage choices interact with outcomes across market conditions.
- **Regret Curve** — A plot of "what would have happened if you held longer / cut sooner."
- **Discipline Index** — A compound score blending plan-adherence, stop discipline, and journaling consistency.
- **Session Band Analysis** — Performance across Asia, London, and New York sessions, with timezone normalization.
- **Funding Cost River** — A flowing visualization of cumulative funding paid or earned.

Each visualization links directly back to the underlying trades, so you can click a spike and land in the journal entry that caused it.

## 🤖 AI Trader Companion

The AI trader is not a signal bot. It is a **reasoning mirror**. Its purpose is to study your historical decisions and replay them under varied assumptions, surfacing the gaps between what you *believed* and what the data *implied*.

### What It Does

- Reconstructs your strategy archetypes from tagged trades.
- Simulates alternate outcomes under different leverage, timing, and exit rules.
- Writes a plain-language critique of each session — calm, specific, and unsentimental.
- Flags cognitive biases: recency bias, sunk-cost persistence, and confirmation loops.
- Optionally operates in paper-trading mode on live data, so you can observe its reasoning without capital exposure.

### What It Does Not Do

- It does not execute live orders on your behalf.
- It does not guarantee outcomes, predict prices, or replace your judgment.
- It does not transmit any of your data outside your self-hosted environment.

The AI trader is a *thinking partner*, not an oracle. Treat its output as a second opinion from a very patient colleague who has read every one of your trade notes — twice.

## 🏗️ Self-Hosting & Integrations

The architecture is intentionally modular. You run the pieces you need; you ignore the rest.

- **Ingestion Workers** — Lightweight processes that pull executions from supported venues.
- **Storage Layer** — A schema designed for time-series trade data with rich metadata.
- **Analytics Compute** — Batch and on-demand jobs that generate insight artifacts.
- **Presentation Shell** — The responsive UI that ties everything together.
- **AI Reasoning Sandbox** — Isolated environment for the companion's simulations.

Integration hooks are documented for those who wish to connect custom alerting, backup routines, or external BI tools. The philosophy: **your data, your pipes, your rules.**
